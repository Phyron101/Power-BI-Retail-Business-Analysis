# Power-BI-Retail-Business-Analysis
## Overview
Analysis of four years of US retail sales ($742K revenue) to find what drives
profit and where discounting erodes margin.

## Tools
Power BI, DAX

## Key Findings
- Chairs are the top profit contributor; Tables generate high revenue but lose money
- Sales peak consistently in September, November and December
- Discounted sales increased volume but reduced profit compared with full-price sales
- Recommendation: cap discounts at 20% on low-margin sub-categories
